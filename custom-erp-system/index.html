Questions

Business & ERP System FAQ

More answers on the FAQ page.

What is a custom ERP system?

A custom ERP is one piece of software, built around your own workflow, that connects sales, purchasing, inventory, production, HR and reporting. Instead of adapting your business to an off-the-shelf package, the system follows your SOP.

How much does a custom ERP system cost in Malaysia?

It depends on the number of modules, users and approval levels, integrations, data migration and whether you need a mobile app. We give a fixed quotation after the planning stage. Starting with one module keeps the first step affordable. See our custom ERP guide for what moves the price.

How long does it take to build?

A focused system with one or two modules is much quicker than a company-wide ERP. The timeline is agreed during analysis and planning, after we have seen how your team works, and we show you the system as it is built.

Can you migrate our existing Excel data?

Yes. We review your current spreadsheets during planning, clean up what needs cleaning and import the records you want to keep, such as customers, items and opening stock.

Will it work with our accounting software?

It can include accounting reports itself, or feed your existing accounting software so your accountant keeps the tools they know. We decide this with you during planning.

We are in KL or Johor. Can you still build it?

Yes. Most of our clients are outside Penang. Requirement sessions, demos, testing and training follow the same process for every client. See areas we serve.
