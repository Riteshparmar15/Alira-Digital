Questions

Mobile Application FAQ

More answers on the FAQ page.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. We build for both platforms, usually as one React Native project so features stay in step, or as a progressive web app (PWA) when an app store listing is not needed.

Should I build an app or a website first?

If customers visit once, start with a website or web platform. If they come back often, need reminders, or you want staff to work on the move, an app is worth it. We will tell you honestly which fits.

Can the app connect to our existing system?

Yes. The app can read from and write to your ERP or platform so orders, bookings and records stay in one place. If we built the system, the app shares the same backend.

Can users pay inside the app?

Yes. We integrate a secure payment gateway for passes, orders, deposits or top-ups, recorded automatically against the user.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

We help prepare the listing, screenshots and builds and publish to the App Store and Google Play under your business account.

What happens after launch?

We stay on for support and maintenance: OS updates, fixes and new features, so the app keeps working as iOS and Android change.
