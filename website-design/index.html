Questions

Website Design FAQ

More answers on the FAQ page.

How much does a website cost in Malaysia?

It depends on the number of pages, content writing, e-commerce features and special interactions. We quote after a short brief, and keep content and design in one project so there are no surprises.

Do you write the content?

Yes. Content creation is part of our website service. We work with you to write and structure the text so it is clear to visitors and to search engines.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site with SEO structure: clean headings, page titles and descriptions, schema markup, a sitemap and fast loading. Ranking also depends on competition and content over time, and we advise on what to add next.

What is GEO and AEO, and do I need it?

Generative and answer engine optimisation means structuring your site so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand and quote it. We include clear answers, FAQs and schema so your business can be described accurately.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Where it makes sense we build on WordPress or include an easy content editor, and show your team how to update text, images and products.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. We review what works today, keep your important pages and search history in mind, and rebuild the site with a stronger design and structure.
