SMEs & factories going from paper and Excel to digital

Production records on paper, stock counted in Excel, orders passed around on WhatsApp. It works until the business grows, then nobody is sure which number is right. We build an ERP system around the way your team already works, so every department updates the same records in real time.

ManufacturingTrading & distributionInventory & warehouseProductionSales & purchasing

Built for Alam Aliran Kualiti, a waste management company that moved from spreadsheets and handwritten records to one ERP system.

Today
  • Handwritten job sheets and receiving forms
  • Stock levels kept in several Excel files
  • Month-end reports compiled by hand
With a system
  • One system for receiving, processing, orders and inventory
  • Real-time stock and order status for every department
  • Reports generated from live data
02 / 04

Service businesses that run on spreadsheets

Travel agencies, logistics and waste management companies, professional firms. The work is a stream of jobs, each with a quotation, people, dates, an invoice and a payment. We turn that stream into a system your staff open every morning, with the numbers already added up.

Travel agenciesLogisticsWaste managementProfessional firmsAgencies

Built for Ezi2Travel: trip planning, participants and accounting in one web system.

Today
  • Colour-coded spreadsheets for trips or jobs
  • Quotations and invoices typed one by one
  • Accounts reconciled at the end of the month
With a system
  • Trips or jobs, people and availability at a glance
  • Quotations and invoices generated from the same record
  • Accounting reports ready when you need them
