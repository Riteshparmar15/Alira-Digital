In short

Alira Digital built a booking system for Aurora Car Rental that staff run from their phones. They see today’s pickups and returns at a glance, filter bookings by date and status, open any booking for the customer’s details and remarks, and check which cars are available, rented out, on contract or out of service. A new booking takes a few taps, with the customer’s IC photo uploaded on the spot.

The brief

Cars go out and come back every day.

A rental fleet moves all day: cars are picked up, returned, sent for service and booked weeks ahead. Aurora’s staff needed one place to see what is going out today, what is coming back, and which car is free for the next customer.

What did Alira Digital build for Aurora Car Rental?

A booking system for the staff, built for the phone. It covers a dashboard of available and booked cars with today’s pickups and returns, bookings by date with pickup and return status, booking details with customer info and remarks, vehicle management with availability, contract and out-of-service flags, and new bookings with the customer’s IC uploaded.

Can staff see which cars are free on a given date?

Yes. Each car shows its booked dates, and staff can switch between available and rented-out cars or search by plate number before taking a booking.

Can Alira Digital build a booking system like this for my business?

Yes. We build booking and fleet systems for car rental, equipment rental and other businesses in Malaysia that hand out and take back assets. We start by learning how your bookings, handovers and returns work today, then design the system around that.
