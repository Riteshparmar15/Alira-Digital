In short

Alira Digital designed and built the GYM Member System for Fitcom Fitness: a branded member app backed by an online platform. Members book group classes, private coaching and training camps, pay with Fit Credits, move up member tiers for bigger discounts and see where they rank in the community. The gym runs classes, trainers and members from one centralized system.

The brief

Running a fitness community, not just a gym floor.

Fitcom runs group classes, private coaching and training camps across more than one location. Every member has a credit balance, a level and a class history, and every trainer has a schedule. The brief was to put all of it in one place members actually enjoy opening.

Booking across branches

Group classes, private coaching and training camps, by day and by location, with limited slots per class.

A credit system

Members top up Fit Credits and spend them on classes, so pricing stays simple at the counter.

Rewards that feel earned

Member levels that unlock better discounts the more a member trains and spends.

Trainer schedules and performance

Who teaches what and when, and how each trainer's classes are doing.

What did Alira Digital build for Fitcom Fitness?

A GYM Member System: a branded member app plus the online platform behind it. It covers class booking for group classes, private coaching and training camps, Fit Credits with top-ups, member tiers with discounts, trainer schedules and performance, and a community leaderboard with class memories.

Can a gym app handle classes across several branches?

Yes. Fitcom's app lists classes by day and by branch, each with its own slot limit, time, intensity and credit cost, and members book any branch from the same app.

Can Alira Digital build a member app like this for my business?

Yes. We build member and booking apps for gyms, studios, clubs and other membership businesses in Malaysia. We start by learning how your bookings, packages and member levels work today, then design the app around that.
