In short

Alira Digital built an enterprise ERP system for Apex Enviro Solutions, an industrial waste management and chemical recycling enterprise operating processing plants in Vadodara and Ahmedabad, Gujarat. It replaced handwritten receiving forms, weighbridge paper receipts, and fragmented Excel stock sheets with a unified cloud system covering receiving, laboratory assay reports, multi-plant drum process workflows, sales orders, automated delivery challans, and GST-compliant real-time inventory tracking.

Before

Every drum was written down three times.

Waste arrived with a handwritten receiving form. Washing and drying went on a paper process record. Then someone typed it all into Excel: one sheet for receiving and delivery by customer, another for stock in and out by item. Stock was only as current as the last person who updated the sheet.

  • Handwritten receiving forms at the gate
  • Paper records for each washing and drying step
  • The same quantities typed again into Excel
  • Stock in and out worked out across several sheets

What did Alira Digital build for Alam Aliran Kualiti?

A custom web-based ERP system for their waste management operations. It covers receiving, process records, test records, sales orders, delivery orders, inventory, reports (inventory, process and diesel), and purchase, overtime and maintenance requests, with stock tracked per plant.

Can a custom ERP replace paper process records and Excel?

Yes. Each paper form and spreadsheet became a screen in the system. Data is entered once and flows to stock, orders and reports, so nobody retypes quantities into Excel.

Does Alira Digital build ERP systems for other industries?

Yes. We build custom ERP systems for manufacturing, industrial, service and trading businesses across Malaysia. The system is designed around your own workflow and SOP rather than a ready-made template.
