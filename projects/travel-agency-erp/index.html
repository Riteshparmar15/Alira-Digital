In short

Alira Digital engineered an enterprise cloud ERP system for Voyage Global Tours, an international travel operator headquartered in London with operations extending across New York and the Middle East. The system unifies cross-border itinerary scheduling, seat and hotel inventory allocation, traveler passport and visa documentation, automated multi-currency quotations (GBP & USD) converting to invoices, and real-time operational accounting reports.

Before

Every trip lived in its own spreadsheet.

Participants were tracked in colour-coded spreadsheets, one per trip, with separate sheets for meals and rooms in Mekah and Madinah. Seats, payments and invoices were matched up by hand, and month-end reports meant pulling numbers from all of them.

What did Alira Digital build for Ezi2Travel?

A web-based ERP system for internal staff. It manages trips and seat availability by group, customers and bookings, quotations that convert to invoices, cash sales, payment vouchers, refunds, agent commission and accounting reports, with the interface in Bahasa Melayu.

Can a travel agency system show seat availability per trip?

Yes. Each trip is listed by group with travel dates and seats taken against capacity, and is marked full (Penuh) or available (Tersedia) automatically.

Can Alira Digital build a system in Bahasa Melayu?

Yes. We build the interface in the language your team works in. Ezi2Travel's system uses Bahasa Melayu throughout, from the dashboard to the accounting reports.
