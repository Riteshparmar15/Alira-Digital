What does Alira Digital do?

Alira Digital is a software house in Vadodara, Gujarat, India that builds custom business systems: ERP systems, online platforms, mobile apps for iOS and Android, and corporate websites. Every system is engineered from scratch around the client's own workflow.

Do you work with businesses outside Vadodara and India?

Yes. We work with clients across India (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Pune) and internationally across the US, UK, Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Our systems are cloud-hosted, and our agile process ensures seamless collaboration wherever you are.

What is a custom ERP system, and does my business need one?

A custom ERP (enterprise resource planning) system puts your inventory, production, sales, purchasing and HR records into one system built around your own SOP. If your team still tracks daily work in Excel, paper forms or chat messages, and each department keeps separate records, a custom ERP usually saves time and reduces errors.

What is the difference between a ready-made system and a custom system?

A ready-made system makes your business follow the software's workflow. A custom system is designed around your workflow, so it has only the features you need and can grow with you. Alira Digital builds custom systems only and does not resell ready-made software.

How much does a custom system cost?

The cost depends on how many modules and workflows the system covers. We quote after a requirement gathering session, so you pay for what your business needs instead of a package of features you won't use.
